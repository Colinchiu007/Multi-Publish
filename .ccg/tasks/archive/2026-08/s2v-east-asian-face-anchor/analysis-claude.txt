## Critical

Spec R3 ("非欧美文化命中…必须包含面孔排除项") conflicts with the constraint "modern 场景不得加面孔负面锚", and design 决策2 ("文化命中优先于 era strong") reads as: non-Euro culture fires anchors even at `era=mixed`. - Fix in `story-context-engine.js:535` `buildGlobalNegativeAnchors(era, culture, strongEra)`: face negatives only when `era === 'ancient'` with `strongEra` (dynasty hit or ≥2 independent signals), never when `era === 'modern'`, and skip when `era === 'mixed' && !strongEra`. Make `era` (not `culture`) the first gate.
- Pin the precedence in one place: brief B constraint line, `design.md` 决策2, and spec R3 must all state "face negative anchors require ancient+strong; culture hit never overrides that".
- T0 test requirement: add a `现代 朝鲜战争/鸭绿江 场景` case asserting `negativeAnchors` has no face terms (and positive EA anchor is still OK).
